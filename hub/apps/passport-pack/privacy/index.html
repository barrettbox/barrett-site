Passport Pack

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Data Not Collected. Passport Pack does not collect personal data from your device for the developer. Vault contents stay on your iPhone under your master password.

Who we are

Passport Pack is developed by Andrew Barrett (Barrettbox). Contact: abarrett879@gmail.com.

What the app stores

Scans of passports, driver’s licenses, visas, and other travel IDs — plus extracted fields — are stored in an encrypted vault on your device (AES-GCM). Scans are captured with the system document camera and are not saved to Photos.

Accounts and servers

There is no Passport Pack account and no developer-operated backend that receives your documents. There are no ads and no third-party analytics SDKs.

Local events

The app may keep a short ring of privacy-safe funnel events on-device (for example: onboarding complete, first scan saved, paywall seen). Those events are not transmitted off-device.

Face ID / Touch ID

Biometric unlock is optional convenience after you create a master password. Face ID does not replace the content encryption key. Biometric data stays on your device and is not collected by us.

Purchases

In-app purchases (Lifetime and Annual) are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.

Password recovery

If you forget your master password, Passport Pack cannot recover your documents. There is no reset email and no recovery server. This is intentional.

Future iCloud backup

Encrypted iCloud / CloudKit backup may ship in a future version. If enabled, only ciphertext would be stored in your private CloudKit database. This privacy policy and App Store nutrition labels would be updated before that ships. Launch builds keep CloudKit backup UI off.

Children

Passport Pack is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect children’s data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.
