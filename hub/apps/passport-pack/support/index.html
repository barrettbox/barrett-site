Common questions

Where are my scans? Inside the encrypted vault — not in Photos. The document camera writes into Passport Pack only.

I forgot my master password. There is no recovery. Documents encrypted under that password cannot be unlocked. This is disclosed in onboarding and Settings.

Face ID stopped working. Face ID only unlocks the app UX after a master password exists. Re-enable Face ID in Settings, or unlock with your master password.

Restore purchases. Use Restore on the paywall or in Settings. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.

Free vs paid. Free: 3 documents, Me profile only. Paid (Lifetime or Annual): unlimited documents, family profiles, local expiry reminders.
